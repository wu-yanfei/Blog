This is my personal blog, **Fly.log**, where I keep notes on tech, research, and experiments.

My research interests are in bioinformatics, with a focus on drug discovery.

The topics are mostly about:

- Linux servers and command-line usage;
- Docker and self-hosted services;
- bioinformatics, transcriptomics data processing, and research tools;
- problems I run into, solutions I try, and notes worth keeping.

This blog also works as my long-term note archive. I hope these notes are useful both for my future self and for others who run into similar problems.
